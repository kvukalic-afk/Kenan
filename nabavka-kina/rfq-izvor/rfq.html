4Structure: please quote both

Option A (our design): folding-carton lid and base, 350 g/m² GC1. Walls are double thickness (outer wall, double crease 0.6 mm gap, inner wall folded inwards), with glue dots on the corner flaps. Our dielines are made for 0.5 mm board. If your board is different, tell us before cutting. Digital print with plotter cutting is fine, or a die if that is cheaper. One die per part is shared by all 6 designs.

Option B (your proposal): rigid two-piece box (greyboard ≈1–1.2 mm wrapped in printed art paper, matte lamination), same outside size, with a paper or black EVA insert. Please quote it if the price is similar.

6Process

  • Step 1: one pre-production sample set on the real board, assembled, with a standard 2 ml vial (Ø16 mm). Send photos and video first, then ship the physical sample.
  • Step 2: after our written approval, print all 6 versions in one run on the same board batch.
  • Step 3: send photos of the finished goods before shipping. Pack boxes flat (Option A) in a strong export carton.
  • Payment: Alibaba Trade Assurance (preferred) or PayPal.

7Acceptance checklist

  • Lid fits the base without force and does not fall off
  • Vial sits firmly in the insert, does not rattle, and is easy to remove
  • Clean cuts and creases; the lamination does not crack on the folds
  • Colours are identical across all 6 versions
  • 4.2 pt text is readable; guilloche lines and micro-text are sharp
  • Labels stay flat on the vial after 24 h in a fridge
